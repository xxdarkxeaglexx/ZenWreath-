Publisher label: ZenWreath · Effective October 11, 2026.

This policy describes the current web game and the proposed offline native edition. Store disclosures must describe the exact binary being submitted. This is not a claim of certification by a store or regulator.

Local game data

Quest saves, appearance, settings, birthday-derived fantasy class, keepsakes, and terms acceptance are stored on your device. Birthday month/day are used to calculate a class; the game does not store the date or request your birth year. Browser or device backups may retain local data under their own settings. You can export your save or erase local game data from Privacy & data in the menu.

Optional signed-in web features

ChatGPT sign-in supplies an account identifier and authenticated identity information to the hosting service. ZenWreath stores the account identifier, chosen nickname, appearance, class, timezone, audio settings, friend relationships, selected weekly sharing preference, quest activity, and any cloud save you choose to enable. The game database does not store your ChatGPT password. OpenAI handles authentication and its own account data under its policies.

Optional wellbeing journal

The web journal can store mood, cravings, sleep, water, cigarettes, drinks, notes, and dates on the game server after you choose to use it. These voluntary entries support reflection and game points; they are not medical monitoring or advertising data. No journal field is required to play. Entries are not shown to other players. Sharing weekly points is separately optional. The proposed offline native edition stores journal entries only on the device and does not sync them.

Friends and rooms

Friends can see your nickname, selected appearance and class, and weekly points only if you opt to share them. Room participants can see gameplay position, actions, appearance, nickname, and original played Forest Flute notes. Room health means in-game hearts, not your medical journal. Room records expire after 24 hours and can be removed when later room operations clean expired records. Do not put private information in your nickname or shared content.

Beta testing and feedback

After accepting the beta tracking notice, beta links record a random browser identifier (or your signed-in account identifier), nickname, invitation type, terms acceptance, first and last visit, sessions, approximate active play time, and the latest game status for each session: realm, in-game position, level, class, health, magic, gems, weapon, difficulty, multiplayer status, and quest progress. This is sampled about every 15 seconds while the beta page is open; it is not a screen recording, precise real-world location, or monitoring outside the game. Optional ratings, opinions, bug reports, and ideas are stored privately for Shane Bradford to review. These are not public reviews. Counts are approximate: one person using multiple anonymous browsers may be counted more than once. We do not collect your precise location or use device fingerprinting for this feature. Records remain until deleted by the operator or associated account deletion. For anonymous record deletion, use the privacy support form and provide the beta browser identifier stored under zenwreath-beta-device in your browser, or request help locating it.

Support and infrastructure

When you submit our support form, we store your supplied reply email, topic, message, submission time, and account identifier if signed in, so the operator can review and respond. Do not send health details or passwords. Requests enter an operator inbox; the form is not live chat. Tickets remain until the operator resolves and removes them, or deletes associated account records.

OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare provide hosting, authentication or storage infrastructure and process network requests. Requests can expose IP address, browser information, security/session information and diagnostics to infrastructure providers. Their retention is governed by their own policies. HTTPS protects transmission to the public service; this policy does not claim end-to-end encryption or that every database/backup is encrypted at rest.

Advertising, tracking and permissions

The game includes no ad network, tracking SDK, or sale of journal entries. It does not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, HealthKit, Health Connect, or body-sensor access. Sharing uses your device’s share sheet or copied links after your action. System providers may process data independently of the game.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you erase app/browser data or use the erase control. Web profile, journal, habits, quest records, and saves remain until you use their deletion controls or delete your ZenWreath account. Account deletion removes the game’s active database records for your profile, cloud save, check-ins, habits, quest history, relationships, membership and associated signed-in support tickets. Shared rooms you hosted or joined close for everyone to remove associated data. It does not delete your ChatGPT account or copies held by other players, exported files, or infrastructure backups outside these controls; provider backup expiry is not verified by this game.

Audience and choices

The proposed store release targets general audiences aged 13 and over and is not a Kids Category submission. Minors should involve a parent or guardian as required by their local law. The game does not verify age. Children should not create an online profile or submit health information without required parental consent; there is no verified parental-consent system implemented here.

You can play solo without a profile, skip journal entries, disable weekly sharing, delete journal entries, export your own data, erase local data, or delete the web account. Use the privacy inquiry form for access, correction or deletion questions. Rights under applicable law remain available.

Updates

Changes to collection or sharing must be reflected here and in the store declarations before release. Operator responsibilities, including privacy inquiries, account support and provider backup handling, continue after publication.
