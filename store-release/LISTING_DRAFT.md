## Description

Carry a little adventure in your pocket. Explore five randomized fantasy realms,
meet grove characters, visit shops and dungeons, recover a lantern’s lost light,
and face monsters and bosses with swordplay, healing and magic.

ZenWreath makes room for a quiet moment: pause whenever you need, visit the
Embercup Café, play original grove-chime notes, and return to a saved quest.

- Mobile touch controls and portrait or landscape play.
- Customizable human, fox, owl, bear and sprite characters.
- Tap to slash; hold and release a charged 360-degree sword spin.
- Directional shields, healing spells and visible health/magic gauges.
- Local autosave and offline gameplay bundled with the app.
- Optional on-device wellbeing reflection journal; no account required.

Native version 1.0 is solo and offline. Online friends, multiplayer and cloud
save belong to the separate web edition and are not included in this release.
There are no ads or real-money purchases in this build. Light is earned game
currency with no cash value.
