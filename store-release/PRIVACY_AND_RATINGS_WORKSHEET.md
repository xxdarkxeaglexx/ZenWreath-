Apple Data Not Collected may be appropriate ONLY after verifying the native binary
sends no data off-device and reviewing the treatment of external support/browser
flows and installed SDKs. Do not reuse that label for the web-enabled edition.
Play Data safety requires the exact app/SDK behavior; on-device-only processing
is different from collection sent off-device. Audit before answering.

Health declaration: optional wellness/reflection functionality is present, even
though the main product is a game. Declare applicable health features truthfully;
do not claim medical-device approval, sensors, Health Connect or HealthKit.

Rating facts to evaluate: fantasy sword combat/monster defeat, bosses with scary
appearance, no gore intentionally included, no sex/gambling/real-money purchases,
optional journal references to alcohol and cigarettes, no native online chat or
UGC. The stores assign ratings; selecting a 13+ intended audience is not a rating
certificate. Web user nicknames/social features need separate moderation review.

Children’s release: not prepared. No verified parental consent, Kids Category
review or Families certification is claimed. General 13+ positioning does not
by itself establish compliance with children’s privacy laws in every territory.
